[04] Process_

5 steps

From brief to final render.

Brief & Discovery

A short call to understand the product, the audience and where the video will live. I'll use the product myself before writing anything.

You get

  • Project scope, timeline and fixed quote
  • A one-page creative brief to sign off

Script & Voiceover

I turn features into a story: problem, product, payoff. Once the script is approved, the voiceover is recorded and becomes the timing for everything else.

You get

  • Script draft with revisions
  • Voiceover options and the final recording

Storyboard & Style Frames

Every scene sketched against the script, plus a few fully designed frames in your brand, so you see the look before any animation starts.

You get

  • Scene-by-scene storyboard
  • 2–3 styled key frames for sign-off

Animation & Sound

The approved frames come to life in After Effects, with UI animation that matches your real product, plus music and sound design.

You get

  • A full first cut to review
  • Music and sound effects mixed with the voiceover

Revisions & Delivery

Feedback rounds on the cut, then final renders for every place the video needs to run.

You get

  • Revision rounds as agreed in the quote
  • Final MP4 renders in the formats you need
