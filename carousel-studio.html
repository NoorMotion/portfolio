Select a keyframe to shape the speed of the move from that key to the next one.

Drag on an empty track to box-select keys, Shift to add. Hold Shift while dragging keys to snap them to the playhead or other keys.

Shortcuts: F9 easy ease · Shift+F9 ease in · Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+F9 ease out · J/K previous/next key · Alt+←/→ nudge one frame · Cmd/Ctrl+A/C/V select all, copy, paste at playhead · Delete remove key · double-click a track to add a key.

Zoom: scroll on the ruler, Alt or pinch over the tracks, = and -, ; toggles frame view. Shift+scroll pans.
